A PRACTICAL GUIDE

Understanding time zones

How do I compare world times?

Add locations to your dashboard to see their current local times, dates and differences from your device. Search for cities and towns or use the built-in city list.

How do I find a meeting time?

The meeting finder ranks one-hour slots by the number of saved locations within weekday 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. local hours. It does not check holidays or personal calendars.

Does the converter account for daylight saving time?

Yes. It uses your browser’s time-zone database for the chosen date. If a local time is skipped or repeated during a clock change, the converter asks you to choose an unambiguous time.

Where does location data come from?

City search, weather, holidays and other insights use third-party providers. Availability and coverage vary; check the source links and local authorities for important decisions.
